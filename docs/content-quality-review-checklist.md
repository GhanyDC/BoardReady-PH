# Content Quality Review Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing any question or preparing a mock exam.

## Per-Question Review

- Question text is clear and asks one main thing.
- Grammar, spelling, and formatting are acceptable.
- Subject is correct for the active exam track.
- Topic is correct for the selected subject.
- All four choices A-D are complete.
- Exactly one correct answer is selected.
- Correct answer is defensible.
- Rationale explains why the answer is correct.
- Rationale helps the reviewer learn, not just memorize.
- Distractors are plausible but not misleading.
- No copied copyrighted content is included.
- No copied review-center hardcopy drill content is included.
- Source type is appropriate.
- Difficulty is appropriate.
- Bloom level is appropriate.
- Status is correct before saving or publishing.

## Publishing Checklist

- Pending reviewer submissions have been reviewed.
- `needs_revision` questions include clear admin feedback in the review workflow when available.
- Rejected questions are intentionally rejected and not accidentally hidden drafts.
- Draft questions are not visible to reviewers.
- Published questions have a valid subject and topic.
- Published questions have four choices and exactly one correct choice.
- Published questions have a rationale.
- Published questions are original or legally usable.

## Coverage Checklist

- Each subject has enough published questions for drills.
- Priority topics have enough published questions for focused practice.
- Published question supply is balanced enough for the active exam track.
- Mock exam builder shows enough available published questions per subject.
- Shortage warnings are resolved before mock exam generation or publishing.
- Archived questions and inactive topics are not being relied on for new reviewer-facing content.

## Reviewer Submission Review

- Reviewer-submitted questions are checked for originality.
- Reviewer-submitted rationales are reviewed before publishing.
- Reviewer-submitted source type is appropriate.
- Questions that need cleanup are marked `needs_revision` when supported.
- Questions that should not be used are marked `rejected`.
- Only publish reviewer submissions after admin verification.

## Legal And Safety Guardrails

- Do not upload files, images, scans, photos, or attachments.
- Do not use OCR or scanning workflows.
- Do not paste copyrighted reviewer materials.
- Do not store copied review-center questions.
- Do not claim or imply guaranteed exam passing.
- Keep BoardReady PH generic for future exam tracks.
